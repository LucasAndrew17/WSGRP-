COMMUNITY HANDBOOK

Rules & Regulations

1. General Community Rules

  1. Treat all members respectfully.
  2. No harassment or discrimination.
  3. No cheating, exploiting or abusing glitches.
  4. No spam or intentional disruption.
  5. Follow authorized staff instructions.

2. Roleplay Regulations

  1. Remain in character during active roleplay.
  2. No RDM or VDM.
  3. No metagaming or powergaming.
  4. Respect ongoing scenes.
  5. Use reasonable and realistic roleplay.

3. Law Enforcement Regulations

  • Follow department chain of command.
  • Use professional radio communication.
  • Follow uniform and vehicle policies.
  • Do not abuse department authority.

4. SAFR and EMS Regulations

  • Maintain professional emergency service roleplay.
  • Communicate clearly during incidents.
  • Coordinate with other departments.

5. Staff Policies

  • Remain impartial and professional.
  • Do not abuse staff permissions.
  • Handle reports fairly.
  • Document disciplinary decisions.

6. Disciplinary Guidelines

Possible actions include verbal warnings, official warnings, temporary suspension and removal from the community. Decisions depend on circumstances and authorized staff review.
